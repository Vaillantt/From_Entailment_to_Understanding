Interpretation notes

The subsampling analysis is strongest for Field Operations because it is the target domain and is balanced by design. Public datasets are complementary stability checks. Stable intervals do not imply uniformly high performance: a metric can be stable because errors are systematic.

For intent recognition, Intent Macro-F1 under stratified subsampling is the main class-balanced metric. For slot filling, Slot Macro-F1 by Intent is the main class-balanced metric, while Slot Micro-F1 remains useful as a frequency-weighted diagnostic.
